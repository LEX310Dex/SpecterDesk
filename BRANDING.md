# SpecterDesk branding

## Name

**SpecterDesk**

Pronunciation: *spek-ter desk*

## Primary title

**SpecterDesk — Private Overlay Workspace for Windows**

## Tagline

**Your screen stays public. Your workspace doesn't.**

## Secondary line

**Your private layer above the desktop.**

## Positioning

SpecterDesk is not just a cursor utility. It is a private desktop layer for presentations and screen sharing: browser tools, notes, cursor exclusion, screenshots, and presentation controls in one compact Windows app.

## Visual direction

- Dark graphite / near-black background
- Cool silver typography
- Muted sage or desaturated cyan accent
- Thin line icons
- No neon gaming aesthetic
- No gradients unless extremely subtle
- Rounded rectangles, generous spacing, low visual noise

## Suggested icon concept

A minimal spectral silhouette formed from two shapes:

1. a rectangular desktop/window outline
2. a single curved ghost-like cutout / negative-space veil

The icon should remain recognizable at 16×16 and 32×32.
